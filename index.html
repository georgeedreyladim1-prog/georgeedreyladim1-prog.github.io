I’m a Technical Virtual Assistant focused on CRM, workflow automation, and reliable operations support. I build practical systems that reduce repetitive work while keeping leads, data, communication, and day-to-day processes organized.

Before transitioning into the Virtual Assistant industry, I spent more than 10 years in the Production and Manufacturing Industry for a multinational company. That experience strengthened my discipline, attention to detail, time management, teamwork, and ability to work accurately under pressure.

Today, I combine that operations mindset with tools such as Zapier, Make.com, n8n, GoHighLevel, Google Workspace, Trello, Asana, and AI platforms. I enjoy understanding how a process works, finding repetitive steps, and turning them into cleaner, more reliable workflows.

My goal is to help business owners spend less time on repetitive work and more time on decisions, customers, and growth.
